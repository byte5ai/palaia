claude.ai

Web, desktop, mobile and Cowork · custom connector on every plan

blocked by your access mode

Locked mode cannot serve this client — and no URL will change that

claude.ai connects from Anthropic’s cloud, not from this device. Your hub answers only inside your tailnet, so the connector would time out whatever you paste into it.

Two honest ways forward

Switch to Cloud mode
Tunnelled endpoint, mandatory authentication, dashboard still tailnet-only. About a minute.
Stay locked, use a local client
Claude Code, Codex and LM Studio run on your machines and reach the hub as it is.

Other limits worth knowing

ChatGPT: writing needs a Business, Enterprise or Edu workspace
On Plus and Pro, connectors are read-oriented. palaia offers a read-only profile so recall works and writes are not silently dropped.
